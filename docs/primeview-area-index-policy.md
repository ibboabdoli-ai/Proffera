# PrimeView area index policy

PrimeView currently keeps 88 customer-approved service-area routes available to visitors and booking flows.

For search indexing, only the 10 bespoke area landing pages are published as indexable pages and included in the PrimeView sitemap. The 78 generated template area pages remain reachable and keep their self-canonical URLs, but use `noindex, follow` until they receive genuinely distinct local content.

## Indexable bespoke areas

- Ealing
- Harrow
- Wembley
- Uxbridge
- Ruislip
- Greenford
- Acton
- Northolt
- Edgware
- Pinner

## Generated coverage pages

All other approved service-area routes remain available for users but are intentionally omitted from the sitemap and marked `noindex, follow`.

This policy is reversible: a generated area can become indexable once its page has meaningful, location-specific evidence and content rather than only a shared template.
