# PrimeView booking route

The dedicated `/boka/primeview` route mirrors PrimeView Window Care branding and keeps the generic Proffera booking templates unchanged.

## Window Cleaning guide estimate

The customer-facing calculator is a non-binding guide only.

- Exterior cleaning starts from the known PrimeView guide of £4–£5 per window.
- Inside & outside uses a provisional £7–£9 per-window guide.
- Commercial properties, third-storey/other access and difficult access can raise the guide range.
- Frames & sills do not add a separate surcharge in the guide.
- Recurring frequency is collected but does not automatically discount the estimate.

The final price is confirmed by PrimeView after reviewing the submitted property details. If PrimeView changes its pricing policy, update both the client calculator and the server-side estimate helper together.
